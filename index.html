FocusPulse v1.0 — Official Release & Features

FocusPulse v1.0 provides a unified productivity and academic management environment. The core application modules are organized in the following order:

1. Smart Pomodoro Timer

Configurable focus, short break, and long break intervals with wall-clock synchronization and preset study modes (Normal, Blitz, Quick Rev, and Extra+).

2. Smart Task Tracker & Active Courses

Deadline-sorted task management organized by category, subcategory, and two-letter course badges, with automatic 24-hour archiving to Completed Task History.

3. Seven-Day Habit Flow Tracker

Interactive weekly check-in matrix featuring customizable weekly frequency targets, active streak tracking, and completion analytics.

4. Active Study Plans & Hydration Tracker

Recurring daily and weekly study schedules paired with daily fluid intake monitoring and scheduled hydration reminders.

5. Zenith AI Coach & Session Workspace

Academic strategy assistant supporting natural-language task and habit commands, accompanied by a dedicated session scratchpad.

6. Account Analytics & Data Backup

Comprehensive productivity metrics, course management, optional cloud synchronization, and local JSON data export and import.

User Feedback & Continuous Improvement

FocusPulse — Application Guide

1. Home & Smart Pomodoro Timer

Initiate focus sessions, switch between Pomodoro, Short Break, and Long Break intervals, or configure custom session durations and presets. The dashboard also displays your upcoming tasks and habits.

2. Smart Task Tracker & Active Courses

Create tasks assigned to specific categories, subcategories, and two-letter course badges. Tasks are sorted automatically by remaining days until the due date, and completed tasks are archived to Task History after 24 hours.

3. Seven-Day Habit Flow Tracker

Record daily habit completions across the seven-day grid, monitor weekly target progress, and review your streak leaderboard.

4. Active Study Plans & Hydration

Maintain recurring weekly study plans and log daily water intake with customizable glass sizes and reminder intervals.

5. Zenith AI Coach & Session Workspace

Consult Zenith AI for structured study planning, task prioritization, or direct command execution, and record session notes in the Quick Study Notes panel or slide-out Workspace.

6. Account Analytics & Settings

Review focus and completion metrics, manage course profiles, configure notifications and interface scaling, or export and import JSON data backups.
